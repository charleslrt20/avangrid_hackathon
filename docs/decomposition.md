Generated by `scripts/06b_decompose.py`. Modeling population 33,927 customers.

## NMF load decomposition

K-Means archetypes (docs/segmentation.md) force every home into one group, but load shapes form a continuum. Non-negative matrix factorisation instead writes each home's shape as a **mix of parts**:

$$S_i \approx \sum_j w_{ij} H_j, \quad w_{ij} \ge 0, \quad \textstyle\sum H_j = 1$$

$S_i$ = customer *i*'s mean load in each of 12 months x 24 hours (288 cells), scaled to sum to 1. Because both $S_i$ and each part $H_j$ sum to 1, $w_{ij}$ is about the share of the home's annual energy that part *j* explains, and $w_{ij} \times$ annual kWh is the kWh it explains. Months with no readings take the customer's own mean for that hour (1.2% of customers have any such cell; 0.24% of cells overall).

**Choice of k (label-free).** NMF is refit separately on odd and even weeks (both halves cover every season) and the two sets of parts are matched (Hungarian assignment on cosine similarity). The rule, set before looking at appliance labels: the largest k ≤ 8 at which *every* part is found again in the other half (cosine ≥ 0.9).

**Reading the parts.**

- **Heating** splits into 2 part(s) (C0 winter 07-16h, C5 winter 23-05h): together 55% of an electric-heat home's energy vs 27% for a typical home, and 43% for labeled HP homes.
- **L2 EV charging has no part** (best AUC 0.58): ~2 blocks of ~3 h a week vanish in monthly averages. L2 detection stays with the event detector (docs/classification.md); NMF complements it, it does not replace it.
- **C3 (year-round 18-23h) ranks PV homes (AUC 0.83) because it is an evening part**: delivered-only metering clips PV homes' midday load to 0, which shifts their *measured* energy to the evening. It is a metering artefact, not an appliance.
- **C6 (summer 22-05h) matches L1 EV charging (AUC 0.83)**, but it peaks in summer, so overnight cooling probably contributes too; the L1 detector itself only uses mild nights.
- **C1 (year-round 07-15h) is unexplained**: closest flag water heater (low conf.) at AUC 0.67, below the 0.75 bar.
- **C2 (summer 13-19h) is unexplained**: closest flag pool at AUC 0.72, below the 0.75 bar.
- **C4 (year-round all day) is unexplained**: closest flag pool at AUC 0.60, below the 0.75 bar.
- **Emerging-load homes** carry more of C1 (year-round 07-15h, +9%) and C6 (summer 22-05h, +8%) than typical homes.

## Exemplar customers per appliance class

One exemplar per class, picked by a fixed **medoid rule** so the plots show a *typical* member, not the most extreme one: among class members with ≥ 350 days of data, not very-high-load and (outside the PV classes) not PV, take the customer closest to the class median on the class's defining features (robust z-score: distance / IQR). Where labels exist (EV, HP, PV) and at least 3 labeled members qualify, the exemplar is drawn from the labeled members. Customer ids are not shown in docs or figures; they are kept in `outputs/exemplars.parquet` (gitignored) for the app.
