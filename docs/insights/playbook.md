# Program playbook (task 2.4)

Generated by `scripts/09_insights.py` from the same facts as the segment cards.

## System context

- Today the system peaks in summer (79.1 MW on Jul 2, 91 °F); the winter peak is 72% of it (57.3 MW on Jan 25).
- It flips to winter-peaking at about 20% heat-pump adoption (range 20%-36% across donor groups). At 30% the winter peak reaches 89.9 MW.
- Batteries must recharge as a coordinated fleet: uncoordinated overnight recharge in a 30% heat-pump winter creates a 108 MW night peak; coordinated recharge brings the annual peak to 84.0 MW.
- Avoided buildout alone does not pay for a battery: a Tier 1 home avoids ≈ $335/yr (NPV ≈ $3,051) at mid placeholder $/kW-yr. Programs should target constrained feeders, electrification headroom and customer value (backup, bills) on top.
- 3 kVA electrification lens: 20% of simulated heat-pump adopters exceed their old peak + 3 kVA (median 12 h/yr); 1 unit brings 66% of them within the cap, 2 units 73%.
