k = 6 chosen for interpretability; silhouette stays ~0.1 at every k, so these are **archetypes on a continuum**, not separable clusters.

- Bootstrap stability (20 resamples): ARI mean **0.905** (min 0.343).
- Interleaved odd vs even weeks (same centroids, profiles rebuilt from each half): ARI **0.690**, same archetype for **86.6%** of customers.
