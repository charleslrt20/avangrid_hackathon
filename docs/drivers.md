# Drivers of load and system peaks (task 1.5)

Generated by `scripts/07_drivers.py`. Modeling population 33,927. Coincident peak (CP) = the system's top 88 hours in summer (Jun-Sep) and in winter (Dec-Feb). A group's CP share is its share of total customer load in those hours; 95% CIs from a Poisson bootstrap over customers (500 draws). Weather → load relationships are causal (weather is exogenous); appliance / segment → peak links are **associations**.

System: summed CP load 65.4 MW summer vs 51.7 MW winter (winter = 79% of summer). System daily load rises 10.9 MWh/day per °F below 50.0 °F and 38.8 MWh/day per °F above 62.5 °F, plus -0.2 MWh/day per °F extra below 20 °F.
